PARTNERS / COLLABORATION

Support the next generation of miniature transit engineering.

TransitOps-M is an independently developed experimental system for fleet dispatch, depot management and guided HO-scale buses. We're building an approachable platform for testing operational decisions and demonstrating engineering to the public.

04 / FUTURE SERVICE MODEL

Designed for
long-term operation.

Our longer-term concept includes leasing miniature bus fleets, upgrading compatible existing exhibits, and offering managed monitoring, control software and maintenance. These are proposed services, not commercially available offerings today.

FLEET LEASING / PROPOSED

Vehicles as a service

Provide, support and maintain compatible miniature vehicles under recurring agreements.

RETROFIT / PROPOSED

Modernize existing systems

Evaluate compatible guide-wire roads, vehicles and electronics for software and control upgrades.

OPERATIONS / PROPOSED

Managed automation

Local-first operations, fleet telemetry, diagnostics and updates under an ongoing service arrangement.
